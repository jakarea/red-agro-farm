## Cross-Check & Sourcing Execution Guide

### 1. Landed Cost Economics & Shipping Strategy
* **Air Consolidation Cargo (Recommended for Test Batches 50–100 units):**
  * Average freight rate from Guangzhou/Yiwu warehouses to Dhaka: **$5.50 – $6.50/kg** (~৳680 – ৳800/kg).
  * Because every product above weighs between **35g and 120g**, unit air shipping costs remain minimal (**৳25 to ৳88 per unit**).
  * Delivery time: 7 to 10 days by air cargo through standard Bangladesh freight forwarders.
* **Consolidated Sea Container (Scale Phase 500+ units):**
  * Freight drops to **$1.20 – $1.80/kg** (~৳150 – ৳220/kg), expanding net margins by an additional **10%–15%**.

### 2. Sourcing Channel Differentiation
* **1688.com vs. Alibaba.com:**
  * **1688.com** provides **20%–40% lower FOB prices** on plastic injection molds, clips, and foam sheets compared to Alibaba export listings.
  * For non-Chinese speakers, working through an export agent or freight forwarder in Yiwu/Guangzhou allows seamless 1688 procurement at domestic factory prices.
  * **Alibaba.com** is viable for initial sample purchases (MOQ 10–50 units) using Trade Assurance for payment protection.

### 3. Localization & Bundling Moat for BD E-commerce
To outperform generic Daraz drop-shippers and overseas cross-border listings:
1. **Bangla Instruction Cards:** Include a printed Bangla quick-start guide (e.g. soil pH meter reading scale, air-layering steps, or self-watering spike setup). This eliminates customer confusion and slashes return rates.
2. **Starter Bundles:** Combine complementary SKUs (e.g. *3-in-1 Soil Tester + Pruning Snips* or *Self-Watering Spikes + Flairosol Sprayer*) at ৳690–৳850 to lift Average Order Value (AOV) above courier break-even.
3. **Short-Form Video Demonstration Ads:** Incumbent Daraz sellers use static manufacturer stock photos. Running native TikTok/Facebook Reel problem-solution videos (as outlined in the table) drives immediate conversion on standalone Shopify/WooCommerce stores.
