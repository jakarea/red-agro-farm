## Filtered out (and why)

- **Live plants, seeds, bulbs** (dominated "rooftop garden" results) — not China-sourcable.
- **Vermicompost, fertilizers, rooting hormone** — local commodity/chemical handling, heavy.
- **Cement/ceramic pots, glass globes** — fragile (exclusion rule).
- **3-pc tool sets at ৳86–130, spray heads ৳43–100, poly bags ৳34–103** — real demand but below the ৳150 price floor; only viable as bundle-fillers.
- **AIRAJ shears ৳1,367, sprayer wands ৳1,383, 40-gal bags ৳415+** — above ৳600 ceiling (noted as upsell headroom).
- **"Agro tools" heavy items (nirani/weeder ৳189, fruit catcher ৳270)** — traction too thin or shape/weight poor for air freight; fruit catcher worth a second look only if pole is sold separately locally.
